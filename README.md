# Content Lead Engine

I want to create a landing page for my agency, which generates leads for clients through their content. We do ads management, and we also do ManyChat automations to qualify their leads through DMs and organize their whole Instagram inbox. That's it.

I don't want to do pricing. I want to have a call to action where they book a discovery call. I also have some stats, but I don't have them written down for now. I'll also want a stat section: who our clients are. From the brochure PDF, you can already see who the clients we're working with are. Regarding numbers, I still don't have that written down, so I'll do it later.

I sent you pictures and brochures so you know what my logo is and what look I'm looking for. I want an Apple-style website, something really professional with some animations when they are scrolling, if possible. That's it.
